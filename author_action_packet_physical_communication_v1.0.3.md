# Author action packet for final submission gate

This packet lists the only actions that require the authors' authenticated access
or explicit approval. The scientific and reproducibility package is already locally
verified.

## 1. Institution portal evidence

Using the school-approved Clarivate JCR/MJL portal, record for ISSN `1874-4907`:

- JCR edition/year
- exact subject category
- JCR quartile
- SCIE status
- access date
- screenshot/export filename or evidence number

Using the school-approved CAS portal, record the corresponding year, category,
quartile and evidence locator. Keep the original screenshot or export beside this
file. Do not infer either quartile from public aggregators.

## 2. Elsevier account check

The corresponding author should log in at <https://submit.elsevier.com/PHYCOM>
and confirm the final selectable article type, author/ORCID fields, upload slots,
declarations, AI-use field, CRediT field and APC/subscription route. No account,
password or manuscript was entered by this workflow.

## 3. Author approvals

- Confirm the candidate postal address: Beijing University of Posts and
  Telecommunications Century College, Kangzhuang Avenue, Kangzhuang Town,
  Yanqing District, Beijing 102101, China.
- Confirm all authors approve the identified manuscript, cover letter, declarations,
  highlights, graphical abstract and v1.0.3 DOI citation.

## 4. Return format

Send back the two portal evidence locators, the confirmed article-type label,
the APC route, and either `address confirmed` or the corrected address. The local
records can then be updated and the readiness state reevaluated.
