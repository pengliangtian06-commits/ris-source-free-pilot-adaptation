# Cover Letter Draft: Digital Communications and Networks

**Manuscript title:** Source-Free Pilot Adaptation for RIS-Assisted Wideband
MIMO Channel Estimation under Explicit Distribution Shifts: Accuracy-Compute
Trade-offs and Failure Boundaries

Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript “[manuscript title]” for consideration as an Original
Research Article in *Digital Communications and Networks*.

The manuscript addresses a deployment problem in learned wireless channel
estimation: a source estimator can degrade after a propagation shift, while
target CSI labels are unavailable. We introduce a reproducible evaluation
protocol for source-free pilot adaptation in an explicit BS--RIS--UE wideband
MIMO simulator. The protocol separates an unlabeled target support block from a
disjoint evaluation block and compares a frozen estimator, a lightweight
adapter, full-model adaptation and least squares across NMSE, QPSK BER,
spectral efficiency, adaptation cost and failure cells.

The main result is deliberately bounded. Pilot-only adaptation recovers the
frozen learned estimator under several tested explicit-RIS shifts, but the
benefit is conditional: least squares remains competitive in BER, small-RIS
beam-squint cells expose failures, and full-model adaptation is more accurate
at a higher parameter cost. A preregistered physical-objective control fails
its independent-contribution gate and is retained as a negative control. This
benchmark/failure-analysis framing is intended to support reproducible
deployment decisions rather than claim universal dominance.

The manuscript includes generated tables, paired uncertainty, a device-aware
reproducibility audit, adaptation-cost metadata and a bounded comparison with
five DOI-verified neighboring records. The work is based on controlled
simulation; the absence of measured-channel validation is stated explicitly as
a limitation. Data/code availability and AI-use declarations are supplied in
the accompanying submission materials and will be finalized with the public
repository decision.

This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have
approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to *Digital
Communications and Networks*.
