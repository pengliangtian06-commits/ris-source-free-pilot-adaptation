## Decision rule

1. Use the institution's stated JCR/CAS/approved-directory rule, including
   subject category, rather than copying a quartile from another category.
2. Retain both JCR and CAS records when they disagree.
3. Reject a candidate only after recording the exact exclusion reason, such as
   non-SCI status, outside-quartile category, APC incompatibility or scope.
4. After selection, replace the generic declaration and cover-letter placeholders
   with the official journal wording and template.
